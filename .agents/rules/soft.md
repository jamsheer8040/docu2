# Soft UI Design System Guidelines

When building or styling user interfaces, strictly adhere to the following "Soft UI" characteristics:

- **Aesthetic Feel:** Weightless, ultra-modern, and premium. It feels sleek and effortless.
- **Shape Language:** Heavily favors pill shapes and exaggerated curves. There are no harsh corners anywhere; everything flows continuously.
- **Borders & Boundaries:** Borders are almost invisible. The inputs don't look like "boxes" drawn on a screen; instead, they use incredibly faint, whisper-thin borders and negative space to define the area.
- **Color & Contrast:** Uses a low-contrast, muted color palette. The icons and placeholder text are a soft, dusty grey. Instead of popping out aggressively, they blend smoothly into the background.
- **Shadows (The "Glow"):** Instead of a traditional harsh drop shadow, primary elements (like blue buttons) use a highly diffused, wide, colored shadow. This creates a "glowing" effect (often associated with Glassmorphism or modern Soft UI), making the button look like it's emitting light rather than just casting a shadow.
